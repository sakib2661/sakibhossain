DUET TechFest 2025 | Line Following Segment

In December 2025, I participated in DUET TechFest 2025 in the Line Following segment. Competing on an unfamiliar track under time and checkpoint pressure made every sensor reading, power rail, and motor response count.

Event Details: December 2025 • DUET TechFest 2025 • Segment: Line Following

Hardware & Equipment Used

Our line-following robot was built around a compact control and power architecture using the following equipment:

  • Buck module LM2569 (regulated step-down power conversion)
  • Boost module XL6000 (step-up voltage support where needed)
  • 2x 3.7V lithium-ion batteries (dual-cell pack powering the system)
  • Arduino Nano (main microcontroller handling sensor reads and control logic)
  • LM298D motor driver (driving the left and right motors)
  • 5-channel digital IR sensor array (detecting track path and line position)

What I Learned

My bot used too much jerking because it was not tuned properly, so I could not pass the second checkpoint.

Excessive jerking on sharp turns and straights disrupts smooth line tracking and wastes momentum on technical sections. Proper PID / speed and steering tuning, smoother motor transitions, and track-side calibration are essential before attempting high-speed checkpoint runs.
